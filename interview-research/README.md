# Accenture final interview: research results

*Compiled 9 October 2026. Scope: **the final interview only** (the round after the online assessments) for fresher and campus hiring in India. The assessments, the communication round and the offer stage are not covered.*

This folder holds what was found when written accounts and Reddit reports of the Accenture final interview were collected and counted: what candidates were asked, how long it ran, and how it was run. All counts below are computed from the data files in [`data/`](data/), not typed by hand.

## The short version

- **It is mostly an HR-and-projects interview.** At least one HR-type question (introduction, projects, teamwork, conflict, why Accenture, strengths, goals and so on) appears in **51 of 54** accounts. In the 20 Reddit reports, **6** say the round was HR or general questions with nothing technical.
- **Two questions are close to universal.** "Introduce yourself" appears in **40 of 54** accounts and a project walk-through in **43 of 54**. In the Reddit reports each appears in 10 of 20 (introduction) and 10 of 20 (projects).
- **"Do you have any questions for us?" is asked a lot** (22 of 54 accounts) and is where several candidates say they were advised never to answer "no".
- **Technical questions exist but are small and basic.** A CS-fundamentals or live-coding question appears in **23 of 54** accounts (9 with coding). 5 of 20 Reddit reports include a technical question, usually basic (object-oriented programming, a basic data-structures question, or a follow-up on the candidate's own project).
- **It is short.** 32 accounts give a length. Most are about 15 to 30 minutes; the shortest recorded is about 7 minutes, and a few multi-round accounts run to 45 minutes or an hour or more in total.
- **One interviewer is the norm**: a single interviewer in 34 of the 54 accounts.

## How the interview is run

| Finding | Evidence | How sure |
|---|---|---|
| **Online (virtual).** | Reports call it an "online HR interview" and a "virtual interview", and one account describes re-entering "the lobby" after the interviewer's video froze. | Reported by several candidates |
| **Waiting is long.** | Waits of about 40 to 45 minutes before the interview are recorded, one candidate started after a wait through the lunch hour, and one waited more than four hours after a 10:30 slot. | Reported by candidates |
| **The interview software is not named.** | No account or report names the video platform used for campus interviews. One account says only that the assessment's coding section ran on the HirePro platform. | Unknown |
| **Typical order.** | The interviewer introduces themselves, you introduce yourself, you explain a project (they may cross-question), then HR-type questions, then your questions. | Described in several reports |

## Method and limits

- **Sources.** Written interview accounts on GeeksforGeeks, LeetCode Discuss, Medium and similar sites, each kept only if the author described their own interview, plus Reddit comments found by scanning subreddit listings through the Arctic Shift archive and reading the comment threads of about 100 interview threads. The archive's keyword search timed out on busy subreddits, so a thread that never mentions Accenture in its title or text could be missed.
- **Who writes these.** Candidates who got far enough to write about it, and people write more when they were selected or upset. Read every count as **direction, not probability**.
- **Excluded on purpose.** Promotional comments advertising interview-practice tools, a post that copied another post, experienced-hire interviews (a different track, summarised separately and not counted), and advice with no interview behind it.
- **Privacy.** No usernames or author names are published here. Reddit text is not reproduced; topics and links are. Anyone who wants a report removed can ask and it will be taken out.
- **Not covered.** The online assessments, the communication assessment and the offer and joining stages.
