# For YOU, the interviewer (do NOT upload this to the AI)
25 likely Accenture final-round questions, from 54 written accounts + 20 Reddit reports (most common first). Ask them in this order, add follow-ups like "why?", "what alternative?", "what would you improve?", then end with "Do you have any questions for us?".

1. Introduce yourself.
2. Walk me through your best project.
3. What exactly was your role in it?
4. Why this tech stack? What alternatives did you consider?
5. Biggest challenge or bug in the project?
6. What would you improve?
7. Tell me about your internship.
8. Teamwork: a conflict, a failure.
9. OOP pillars with examples.
10. DBMS: keys, joins, normalization, ACID.
11. OS: process vs thread, deadlock.
12. Networks: HTTP vs HTTPS, TCP vs UDP.
13. Array vs linked list; stack vs queue.
14. SQL: second-highest salary; GROUP BY.
15. One easy coding question (reverse a string, first non-repeating character).
16. What is REST? JWT? CORS?
17. What is RAG? What do you use GenAI for?
18. Cloud basics: IaaS/PaaS/SaaS, AWS S3/EC2.
19. Your certifications: what did you learn?
20. Patents and open source: what was your part?
21. Strengths and weaknesses.
22. Why Accenture?
23. Relocation and shifts?
24. How would you learn a new technology in a month?
25. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

Trap questions worth asking (they test honesty): "Is your RAG chat really multi-turn?" · "What accuracy did CaneGuard get?" · "What if the image model finds nothing?" · "Which WasteZero files did you write?" · "Is your patent granted? Published?" · "Is your toll VDF a real VDF?" · "Is it production-ready?"
