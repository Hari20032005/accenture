## PATENT A: toll system (App. No. 202641056093; co-inventors Dr. Priya V, N Sakthivel, C Edward, me)
Filed title: *Secure Toll Management System With Cryptographic Authentication And Transaction Sequencing* (the invention-disclosure form calls it "Hybrid Toll Management System (HTMS) with Self-Healing Trust Governance and Tamper-Evident Transaction Sequencing"). My contribution = F4.
- **Problem:** FASTag/RFID toll systems are centralized, with weak checks on the reader devices, and are open to replay attacks, rogue or compromised readers and tampered records.
- **Reader side:** RFID reader (ESP8266/ESP32 + MFRC522, 13.56 MHz) hashes the tag UID with SHA-256, builds an event {hashed UID, reader ID, timestamp, nonce}, signs it with HMAC-SHA256 using a per-device key, and buffers up to 10 events if the network drops.
- **Backend (FastAPI) checks:** signature, nonce uniqueness (replay), timestamp drift, request rate. Then trust check, fraud check, approval.
- **Trust governance:** each reader has a trust score 0–100; penalties (auth failure −40, balance manipulation −30, replay −15, abnormal volume −12, rate breach −10); below a threshold (example 35) the reader is quarantined. **Self-healing recovery in 3 stages:** time-decay `recovery = 2.0 × ln(1 + hours)` capped (example 80); graduated probation challenges (KNOWN_TAG, TIMING_CHECK, HASH_VERIFY); peer consensus (at least 2 voters, at least 60% approval, no self-vote). Cross-reader suspicion: tags recently scanned by a quarantined reader get stricter fraud checks for a while.
- **Hybrid fraud detection:** rules + supervised tree-based ML + anomaly detection combined into one fraud score (features: amount, speed, inter-arrival time, sin/cos of hour); decision = approve, flag or reject.
- **Tamper-evident sequencing:** each approved transaction is chained: `vdf_input = SHA256(prev_output + event_id + tx_hash + reader_id + timestamp)`, `vdf_output = SHA256^1000(vdf_input)`. Changing, inserting, deleting or reordering any record breaks the chain, detected per link. Batches are summarised in a **Merkle tree** and only the **Merkle root** is anchored on a blockchain (tested on a local Ganache Ethereum network), so on-chain storage stays small.
- **Evidence (all from simulation/prototype, say so):** 48 automated tests passed (2.95 s); simulated dataset of 10,000+ transactions, 5 tags, 3 simulated readers; 1000-iteration chain step under 50 ms; example trust recovery 60 → 73.8 after 24 h; on the simulated data the hybrid ensemble scored accuracy 0.95, precision 0.92, recall 0.89, F1 0.90, versus 0.92/0.88/0.85/0.86 for the supervised model alone. A hardware prototype (ESP8266 + RFID + LCD "Access Granted/Denied") exists. **Never claim real toll plazas, real traffic or deployment.**
- **Honest challenge to expect:** a true VDF has fast verification; ours is 1000 sequential SHA-256 hashes, which is a sequential hash chain whose check recomputes the work. Say so if asked, and that it still gives ordered, tamper-evident records.

## PATENT B: federated PINN for semiconductor quality control (App. No. 202641065392; with Dr. E.P. Ephzibah)
Applicant VIT; specification dated 5 May 2026; 10 claims (a method claim plus dependents). My contribution = F4.
- **Problem:** detect anomalies in semiconductor fabs (India's PLI-scheme fabs) early, explain them physically, share learning across fabs without sharing raw wafer data, and automate compliance reporting.
- **Four layers:** (1) sensors on the fab line; (2) a **digital twin** (India-Adapted Digital Twin Schema, extending IEC 63278 Asset Administration Shell in JSON-LD) holding monsoon-humidity, grid-quality (voltage sag/swell) and supplier-quality-variance data, synced every 100 ms; (3) **federated layer**; (4) **compliance layer** mapping anomalies to SEMI E10/E35/E116 and auto-generating the PLI audit XML.
- **Model:** an **LSTM-autoencoder** learns normal behaviour; high reconstruction error = anomaly. Physics terms are added to the loss: `L_total = L_recon + 0.1·L_Rayleigh + 0.1·L_Electromigration + 0.1·L_Diffusion`. Rayleigh limit `CD = k1·λ/NA = 0.40 × 193 nm / 0.93 = 83 nm` (193i lithography); Black's equation for electromigration (n=2, Ea=0.8 eV, T=378 K, Jmax 2.5×10⁶ A/cm²); Fick's diffusion (erfc profile). **Why:** physics terms stop physically impossible predictions, reduce the data needed, and tag *which law was violated*, so anomalies are explainable. Edge inference on a Jetson Orin NX under 100 ms.
- **Federated part:** each fab trains locally; **FedProx** (proximal term μ=0.01) keeps local models close to the global one; **Gaussian differential privacy** (ε=1.0, δ=10⁻⁵, σ=1.08, per-round ε=0.33) noises the updates; **INT4 quantization** cuts bandwidth 8× (32-bit to 4-bit); a coordinator (Flower) aggregates every 4 hours.
- **Outputs:** anomaly score + physics-violation tags → SEMI classification → updates MTBF, cost-of-ownership ledger and OEE → severity decides Grafana alert + PagerDuty or just an InfluxDB log.
- **Figures in the specification (defect-injection validation, i.e. simulation, worded "may"):** mean detection 6.6 h vs 17.8 h for periodic inspection (63% faster); physics constraints may cut training-data needs by at least 33%. **Never claim a real fab deployment or real-world accuracy.**
